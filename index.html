How to use this page

Kasatria People Visualization is an interactive 3D way to browse members: each tile is one person, colored by net worth, arranged into a Table, Sphere, Helix, or Grid shape you can rotate and explore.

Moving around

  • Left-click + drag: rotate the view - on the Helix, this is also how you swing around the coil to bring its other strand into view
  • Right-click + drag: pan the view
  • Scroll wheel: zoom in/out on Table, Sphere, and Helix. On Grid, it instead pages through the depth layers one at a time - scroll all the way to move all the way to the back; hold Ctrl/Cmd while scrolling (or pinch on a trackpad) to zoom Grid instead
  • Arrow keys or the on-screen arrow pad: move/rotate - behaves a bit differently per shape (Table pans, Sphere/Helix rotate, Grid steps through depth layers)
  • FIT (bottom-right): fills as much of the screen as possible with the shape, without overlapping the buttons
  • RESET: snaps back to a clean, centered view of whatever shape is active
  • Click a tile: opens that person's full profile

Toolbar buttons (top-right)

  • Manage Data: opens the Google Sheet directly - add, edit, or remove people there
  • Refresh: after editing the Sheet, click this to load your changes here - no page reload or re-signing in needed
  • Insights: a quick summary of the current data (totals, averages, top categories)
  • Sign Out: ends your session

Good to know

Switching between Table / Sphere / Helix / Grid automatically re-centers the camera. Tile colors show net worth: red (below $100K), orange ($100K-$200K), green (above $200K). The Helix is a true double helix - two strands wound around the same axis, 180° apart - so rotating it swaps which strand is currently facing you. Every tile is a solid, two-sided card - the side facing you is full strong color, the side facing away is a dimmed version of the same color - so the shape itself reads as 3D without relying on distance alone. On Sphere, Helix, and Grid, tiles closer to you also fade slightly as they recede, adding to that depth cue.
